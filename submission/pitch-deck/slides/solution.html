THE SOLUTION

A multi-agent AI system that senses, reasons, and acts on air quality — not another dashboard

Sense

Real live air quality and weather data pulled per city, plus real Earth Engine satellite imagery — not seeded mock numbers.

Reason

Five Gemini-powered agents built on Google's Agent Development Kit — parallel analysis, then a critic that checks and revises the synthesis.

Act

Simulates interventions before they're tried, and proactively drafts a health advisory the moment air quality turns hazardous.

This is the "so what" slide — three verbs, sense/reason/act, map directly to the three things a judge will actually see in the demo: real data, the live agent trace, and the what-if panel plus the alert.
