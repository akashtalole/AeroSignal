THE PROBLEM

India's air is a daily, invisible health emergency

1.67M

deaths in India in 2019 were attributable to air pollution

Source: Lancet Planetary Health, 2020 — India State-Level Disease Burden Initiative

Most cities, most days

exceed WHO air quality guidelines — not a once-a-year crisis, a daily baseline.

Response is reactive

authorities act after AQI spikes, with no way to simulate which intervention actually helps.

Tools stop at dashboards

existing apps show a number. Almost none reason about it, simulate a fix, or alert someone.

Open on the scale of the problem: 1.67 million deaths a year in India alone, from the Lancet's own India-specific disease burden study — a real, citable figure, not an estimate we made up. Then pivot to the gap: everyone has an AQI app, nobody has a decision-support system.
